Present-day Hungarian is known for its prolific preverb system. However, this has not always been the case. In the Old Hungarian period, only a handful of preverbs existed (e.g. *meg* ‘perfective’, *el* ‘away’), and their occurrence in surviving texts is sporadic. This project set out to explore how the expansion of the preverb system reached its present state, in which lexical items like *tönkre* ‘to ruin’ or even *szénné* ‘to coal’ may function as preverbs. The research took advantage of advances in Natural Language Processing (NLP) technology and was based on the automatic analysis of large bodies of text. The project resulted in open-access datasets that can be browsed on this website, giving everyone the opportunity to embark on linguistic time travel and explore the changes for themselves.

- **Title:** *The emergence of preverb constructions in Hungarian: a corpus-driven approach*
- **Funding institution:** [National Research, Development and Innovation Office](https://nkfih.gov.hu/about-the-office)
- **Project ID:** PD-142317
- **Principal investigator:** [Ágnes Kalivoda](https://kagnes.github.io/)
