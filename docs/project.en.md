Present-day Hungarian language is known for its prolific preverb system. However, this has not always been the case. In the Old Hungarian period, only a handful of preverbs existed (e.g. *meg* ‘perfective’, *el* ‘away’) and their occurrence in surviving texts is sporadic. This project aimed to explore how the preverb system’s expansion reached the present state, when lexical items like *tönkre* ‘to ruin’ or even *szénné* ‘to coal’ may function as preverbs. The research took advantage of the advances in Natural Language Processing (NLP) technology and it was based on the automatic analysis of large bodies of text. The project resulted in open-access datasets which can be browsed on this website. This gives everyone the opportunity to start a linguistic time travel and explore the changes for themselves.

- Title: *The emergence of preverb constructions in Hungarian: a corpus-driven approach*
- Financer institution: [National Research, Development and Innovation Office](https://nkfih.gov.hu/about-the-office)
- Project ID: PD-142317
- Principal investigator: [Ágnes Kalivoda](https://kagnes.github.io/)
